Test Case Generation Instructions

This document contains the complete list 
of Business Rules, Specifications, and Validation Requirements for each module.

Objective

Generate comprehensive test cases for the selected target module by reviewing
and incorporating all applicable Business Rules and Specifications defined for that module.
Interact with the UI and Generate Do not Hallucinate for remove or add New Scenraios.

Requirements
Review all Business Rules associated with the selected module.
Ensure every Business Rule is covered by at least one test case.
Do not skip, ignore, or partially cover any Business Rule.
Include both positive and negative validation scenarios where applicable.
Cover  business validations, dependencies, permissions, workflows, calculations, and 
system behaviors defined in the specifications.
Verify edge cases and boundary conditions wherever relevant.
Ensure complete traceability between Business Rules and generated test cases.
If multiple Business Rules apply to a single scenario, reference all applicable rules in the test case.
The final test suite should provide full functional coverage of the module.
